# Site attention path

Status: approved. 8 Oct 2026. Does not change the site, the specs, or the copy.

The live section order stays as it is, and no reorder is proposed.

## The thread

Three things carry the eye down the page. All three are already in the site.

1. **One line.** A thin rule in the signal color (`#5B98DC` at night, `#1A5694` by day). It starts under the hero and ends at the WhatsApp button.
2. **One typeface for numbers.** Big Shoulders is used only for numerals and codes: 30, 01–04, 1990s, AQB, AMM, GCC, GLB. When the eye sees that face, it knows it has reached a fact.
3. **One action color.** The terracotta fill appears only on the hero buttons and in the contact band. Nothing between them uses it, so when it comes back it reads as the destination.

## First screen

The hero exit changed on 8 Oct 2026, evening. The tabs and the mode codes are off the first screen. The last thing the visitor sees is the signal line leaving the photograph toward 30+. Terracotta sits on "Discuss your shipment." AQB first appears on the service teasers.

**Passes across:** the signal line, which drops out of the hero's lower edge.

## Credibility

**The eye lands on "30+".** It is the first large Big Shoulders numeral, and it sits where the line arrives.

The four values (Reliability, Speed, Safety, Professionalism) sit beneath it as a quiet row of four equal columns.

**Passes across:** the four-column grid. The service teasers below use the same column edges, so the eye drops straight down. The values are not tied to any service, because that would be an invented claim.

## Service teasers

**The eye lands on "01" and "AQB" on the sea teaser.** It is the first column, at the start edge.

The order follows the site: 01 sea, 02 air, 03 land, 04 customs. Codes appear only where the facts list one (AQB, AMM). If the hero allows choosing a mode, that teaser could carry the signal tint on arrival. That is a proposal for the room, not a requirement.

**Passes across:** the numerals 01–04. The handover steps reuse the same counting style, so the visitor reads them as a second sequence of four.

## Handover

**The eye lands on step 01, route and spec consultation.**

The signal line returns here, running through the four steps and ending at step 04, direct delivery and handover. This is the one section where the line is the subject, not a guide.

**Passes across:** the line's end point. It stops at "handover" and starts again as the heritage timeline.

## Heritage

**The eye lands on "1990s".** It is the first period on the timeline.

The periods run 1990s, 2000s, 2010s, Present. "Since the 1990s" is the only claim. The "30+" from credibility is echoed here without being restated.

**Passes across:** "Present". It sits at the end of the timeline, closest to coverage, as a hint that the next section shows where the company is today.

## Coverage

**The eye lands on "AQB".** It is the code the visitor already met on the sea teaser.

AMM follows. Then GCC and GLB widen the reach, in the order they appear on the site. Seeing AQB and AMM again tells the visitor this is the same network, not a new set of claims. No ports, borders, or airlines beyond the facts file are added.

**Passes across:** the line. It leaves GLB, the last code, and runs into the contact band.

## Contact band

**The eye lands on the terracotta "Discuss your shipment" button.** It is the first terracotta since the hero.

The line ends against the button's edge. Hours and email sit at the muted text level. WhatsApp stays primary, with no email gate.

## Rules that hold across sections

- **Who animates what:** CSS only, or GSAP for the line. One writer per property.
- **Reduced motion:** the line, numerals, and codes are drawn statically in place. Nothing is hidden behind a reveal.
- **Mobile:** the line becomes a vertical rail at the start edge. The four columns stack, but numerals keep their order. No pins below `md`.
- **Arabic:** the rail moves to the right edge. Only alignment changes. Stills do not mirror.
- **Skins:** night and day each have their own role colors. Nothing is inverted to fake the other skin.
- **Excluded:** Camera C, equirectangular plates, the boxed crane, a globe, a weather map, a second action color, and any new number.
