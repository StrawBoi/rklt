# Section card

The parent writes this file. Subagents do not.

```markdown
# <section>

Status: proposal. Not approved. Does not change the site.

## Attention
One sentence from the site card about what this section must make the visitor notice.

## Animation
Recommended idea, its tier, the eye order, and the reduced-motion fallback.
Alternates: at most two, one line each.

## Visual
Medium, what is in the frame, what stays out, where type sits, night and day.
Prompt: the visual ideator's prompt for the recommended idea only.

## Text
| Slot | EN | AR | Max | Fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Critique
Five lines or fewer from animation, visual, and copy. What they disagreed on.

## Critic
Pass or fail, and the rule if it failed.

## Verdict
The mastermind's pick, in three sentences. What the user is being asked to approve.
```
